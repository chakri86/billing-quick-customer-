# Google Play Reviewer Access Instructions

Quick Customer does not require a remote account or subscription.

On a fresh installation:

1. Open the app.
2. The **Create shop owner** screen appears.
3. Enter any username containing at least three characters.
4. Enter any display name.
5. Enter a password containing at least eight characters.
6. Tap **Create owner account**.

The locally created owner receives Super User access and can review Billing, Sales, Expenses, Inventory, Products, Users, Settings, privacy controls, and printer configuration.

Bluetooth receipt printing is optional and requires a physical paired ESC/POS printer. All other workflows can be reviewed without printer hardware.

No OTP, external approval, paid membership, location restriction, or server-side activation is required.
