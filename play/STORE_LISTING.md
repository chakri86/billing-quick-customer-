## Full description

Quick Customer is an offline-first billing application designed for cafés, tea shops, snack counters, and other small businesses.

Create bills quickly on an Android phone or tablet, even when the internet is unavailable. Products and prices can be changed at any time without changing historical bills.

Key features:

- Fast, touch-friendly phone and tablet billing
- Cash, UPI, and card payment recording
- Cash received and automatic change calculation
- Owner-managed UPI QR image
- Products, prices, categories, and custom Misc items
- Category ordering for frequently used sections
- Super User, Admin, and Employee access roles
- Daily, 7-day, 30-day, all-time, and custom-date reports
- Bill details, cancellation controls, and audit records
- Expense entry, approval, and custom expense dates
- Hybrid inventory for packaged goods and recipe ingredients
- Product-level revenue, cost, and profit reporting when costs are configured
- Optional generic 58 mm and 80 mm Bluetooth ESC/POS receipt printing
- Local data deletion from the app settings

Quick Customer stores operational data locally on the device. The current release does not require a Quick Customer cloud account or send shop records to a Quick Customer server.

Printer compatibility depends on the printer's ESC/POS and Bluetooth Serial Port Profile implementation. Always perform a test print before using a printer in live billing.
